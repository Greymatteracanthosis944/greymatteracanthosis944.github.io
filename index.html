🧠 cliplite - Save your clipboard history forever

Download cliplite for Windows

Computers hold only one item in your clipboard at a time. If you copy new text, the old text vanishes. Cliplite solves this problem. It records everything you copy and keeps it in a searchable list. You can find past links, codes, or text fragments in seconds. This tool makes your workflow faster and helps you keep track of your digital work.

📋 How it works

Cliplite runs in the background of your computer. It monitors your clipboard automatically. When you press the standard copy command on your keyboard, the application saves the data. It stores this information on your local drive for privacy. You can open the interface at any time to see your history or copy an old item back to your clipboard.

⚙️ System Requirements

Cliplite works on Windows 10 and Windows 11. Your computer needs at least 200MB of free hard drive space. The application uses very little memory, so it will not slow down your daily work. You do not need administrative rights to run the basic features of this tool.

🚀 Getting Started

Follow these steps to set up the software on your machine:

  1. Visit the official releases page to download the installer file.
  2. Locate the file in your downloads folder after the transfer finishes.
  3. Double-click the file to start the installation.
  4. Follow the prompts on your screen to complete the setup.
  5. Launch the application from your desktop or the start menu.

🛡️ Privacy and Safety

Your data stays on your computer. Cliplite does not send your clipboard information to the internet. We value your privacy and design the tool to function without cloud storage. You remain the only person with access to your clipboard logs.

🛠️ Managing Your History

You can manage your history list through the main panel. Use the search bar to find past items by keyword. If you want to clear your history, click the settings menu and choose the delete option. You can also pin specific items to keep them at the top of the list so they never get lost.
